Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy stored in carbohydrates. It is the primary entry point of energy into the biosphere. The light-dependent reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes and depend on a pigment called [[chlorophyll]].
