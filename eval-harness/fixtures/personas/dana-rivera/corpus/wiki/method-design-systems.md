A design system is worth it exactly when the a11y and consistency cost of NOT having one
exceeds the overhead. On [[work-health-portal]] it paid off; on a one-off marketing page it
wouldn't. It's an instance of [[philosophy-boring]] — reuse the proven component. Parent:
[[profile/overview]].
