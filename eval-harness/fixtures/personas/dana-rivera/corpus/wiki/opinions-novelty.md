Most "innovative" interactions are a designer entertaining themselves at the user's expense.
I've killed my own clever ideas more than anyone else's. The counterweight is [[philosophy-boring]].
The one time novelty is right: when the old pattern actively lies to the user. Parent: [[philosophy]].
