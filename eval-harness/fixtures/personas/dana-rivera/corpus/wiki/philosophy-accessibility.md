I design for screen readers and keyboard first; if it doesn't work there it isn't done. On
[[work-health-portal]] the patients who needed the portal most were the ones on old devices
with large-text settings — the "edge case" WAS the user. Accessibility isn't a checkbox at
the end; it's a constraint that makes the whole design better. Parent: [[philosophy]].
