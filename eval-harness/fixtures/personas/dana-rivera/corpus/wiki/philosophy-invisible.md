The best interface I ever shipped is one nobody remembers using. On [[work-ledger-app]] we
cut onboarding from eleven screens to four and drop-off fell ~40%. Not because the four were
prettier — because there was less to trip over. Polish is what you add when you can't figure
out what to remove. Parent: [[philosophy]].
