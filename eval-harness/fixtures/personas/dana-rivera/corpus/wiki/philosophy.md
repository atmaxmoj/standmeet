Three things I keep coming back to, each earned the hard way. Good design is invisible —
it removes friction rather than adding polish: [[philosophy-invisible]]. Accessibility is
the baseline, not a feature: [[philosophy-accessibility]]. And I reach for boring, proven
patterns over novelty almost every time: [[philosophy-boring]]. They're mid-career opinions:
held firmly enough to act on, loosely enough to be argued out of.
