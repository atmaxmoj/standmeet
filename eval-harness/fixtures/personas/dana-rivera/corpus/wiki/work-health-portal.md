A portal for lab results and appointments. The hard part wasn't the happy path — it was that
our real users were older, on old devices, with large-text and screen readers. This is where
[[philosophy-accessibility]] stopped being theory. We rebuilt on a proper design system so the
a11y work didn't have to be redone per screen: [[method-design-systems]].
