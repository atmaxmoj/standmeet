practical stuff interviewers and recruiters always ask, writing it down so I'm
consistent.

current comp at Orbit: ~$165k base, smallish equity that's probably worth little,
10% target bonus that pays out around 7%. so call it ~$180k total, US, Austin.

expectations: I'd like to get to $190–210k base for the right role. I'm not
purely chasing money — I've said I'd take a lateral for real growth — but I'm not
going to take a pay cut either, I have a mortgage. equity I treat as lottery
tickets, I value base and stability of base.

location: Austin. I'm open to remote (Orbit is hybrid, I go in twice a week and
honestly like it). open to roles in other US hubs if remote, not looking to
relocate physically right now — wife has a job here, we just bought a place.

work authorization: US citizen, no visa needed. (people ask.)

notice period: two weeks, standard. I'd want to leave Orbit on good terms,
they've been fine to me.

timeline: not in a rush, no exploding offer pressure, I'm employed and picky. I'd
rather wait for the right thing than jump. that said if a process drags for two
months I lose interest.

dealbreakers: no pure-frontend roles, no pure-management track (I want to stay
technical for now), no crypto, no defense. mild preference against very early
stage again after the FlowPay layoff, but a great Series B could change my mind.
