ok just dumping this so it's somewhere. why am I leaving Orbit.

real answer: I've stopped growing. integrations work is 80% dealing with other
companies' bad APIs and 20% actual engineering and the 20% is shrinking. I'm
good at it which is exactly the problem, it's comfortable and I can feel myself
getting slower/rustier on the hard stuff.

money is a factor but not the main one. orbit pays fine. I'd take a lateral or
even slightly less for work that actually stretches me. I want to get to senior
at a place where senior means something, not just "has been here a while."

the thing I don't say out loud in interviews: I'm a little scared I waited too
long. five years in and I'm "solid mid" not "obviously senior" and some of that
is I took the safe FlowPay->Orbit move instead of the scary growth one. don't
want to make that mistake again.

what I actually want in the next job:
- real distributed systems problems, scale I haven't seen
- a team where I'm not the most experienced person, where I can learn from people
- less glue code, more design
- ideally still backend, still Go or willing to learn something new like Rust

what I don't want: another integrations/glue role, a place where I'm the senior
person by default, pure on-call firefighting with no building.
