This is the half of my experience that isn't tied to one company: the patterns and
concepts I picked up doing the work, the ones I now reach for by default. Each of
these I learned the hard way on a specific project, and I've linked back to where.

I'm a mid engineer, so treat these as "things I've used in anger and can reason
about," not "things I could write the textbook chapter on." Where my understanding
gets fuzzy, I've said so.

The entries below: rate limiting, idempotency, delivery semantics, retries and
dead-letter queues, cache invalidation, reconciliation, shadow-mode migrations,
isolation levels, distributed locks, backpressure, and eventual consistency.
