I'm a mid-level backend engineer looking for the next step. Not a moonshot title
jump — a place where I can grow toward senior by working on harder problems with
people I learn from.

Concretely: a backend or platform role, Go or a language I can pick up, ideally
somewhere correctness matters (payments, infra, data), because that's the work
I've enjoyed most. I want code review that pushes me, real ownership of a service
or two, and on-call that's humane.

What I'm moving away from: I took the Orbit job partly for stability after a
layoff (see [[FlowPay — Backend Engineer (2021–2023)]]) and I've realized I traded away some growth. I'd
rather be a little uncomfortable and learning.

What I'm honest about: I'm mid-level. I execute well and I'm reliable, but I'm not
going to walk in and architect your platform. I'm looking for the role where I
become the person who can.
