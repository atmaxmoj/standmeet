I think there's value in being clear about where you are. I'm mid-level: past
needing hand-holding, not yet the person who sets technical direction for a team. I
ship features and services reliably, I'm a safe pair of hands on-call, and I know
the shape of what I don't know.

Day to day that looks like: I can take a reasonably-scoped problem and deliver it
well, I ask for help at the right time instead of grinding alone for days, and I'm
useful in code review on the things I know. What I'm not yet: the person who
decomposes a vague, ambiguous problem into work for several people, or who owns the
architecture of a system end to end.

I'd rather state that plainly than oversell and disappoint. The whole point of the
next job is to close that gap — more ambiguity, more ownership, better people to
learn from.
