Orbit is a ~200-person B2B SaaS company selling workflow-automation software to
mid-market companies. I joined in 2023 for more money and a more stable
environment after FlowPay's layoff. I'm on the integrations team.

What I do: build and maintain the connectors that sync data between Orbit and
third-party systems — Salesforce, HubSpot, NetSuite, a dozen others. It's Go for
the core sync engine, some Python for the data-transform scripts, Postgres,
Redis, and a fair amount of "this vendor's API is undocumented and rate-limits
us randomly" detective work. I also built our notification pipeline (see
[[Notification Pipeline (Orbit)]]).

Honest assessment of this job: it's comfortable and I'm good at it, but I've
plateaued. The work is more about gluing APIs together and handling edge cases
than designing systems. The hardest problems are other people's bad APIs, not
interesting engineering. I'm the person people ask when an integration breaks,
which feels good, but I'm not learning the things I want to learn — distributed
systems at scale, real performance work, designing something from scratch.

That plateau is the main reason I'm interviewing. I don't dislike Orbit; I've
just stopped growing here, and I can feel myself getting further from the kind of
deep technical work I'd need to make the jump to senior at a stronger company.
