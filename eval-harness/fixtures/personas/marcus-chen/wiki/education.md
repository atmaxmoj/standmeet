BS in Computer Science from Riverside State University, graduated 2019. It's a
solid state school, not a target school — no FAANG recruiters showed up on
campus. GPA was 3.2; I was the kind of student who did well in the classes I
cared about (operating systems, databases, networking) and coasted through the
ones I didn't (theory of computation, the math-heavy electives).

I didn't do research or a thesis. I did two internships: one at a local
healthcare IT company writing internal CRUD tools in C#, and one at a slightly
bigger company doing QA automation in Python. Neither was glamorous but the
second one is where I learned to actually read other people's code and write
tests.

Honestly, most of what I know I learned on the job, not in school. School taught
me enough vocabulary to be dangerous and the discipline to read documentation. I
sometimes feel the gap with people who came up through more rigorous programs —
they reach for the right data structure or the right concurrency primitive
faster than I do. I've closed some of that gap by reading, but not all of it.
